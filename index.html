Experiences

Here are some of my most valuable experiences that have shaped my engineering journey so far.

Engineering Internship at Triocon Engineering

I worked at Triocon Consulting Engineers to get a feel for the consulting world. At Triocon, I worked in the mechanical department doing HVAC calculations, but primarily spent my time in the electrical department where I worked on floorplans of the electrical systems of the entire building. This consisted of doing wiring layouts, DB board load balancing, and wiring layouts for an automated smart lighting system.

Maverick International

This was some of the most fun work I have done. At Maverick International, a packaging machine company, I worked on wiring two PLC panels for two sections of one of their massive machines. I had to follow blueprints and work on placing, wiring, and labelling the panels to a production grade. I also got to see some PLC programming. This experience really opened my eyes to how mechanics and electronics can intersect to create a smooth running machine.
